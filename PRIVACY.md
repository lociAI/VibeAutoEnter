# Privacy Policy for VibeAutoEnter

**Last Updated:** November 2026

**VibeAutoEnter** ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by **LociaX Software**. We value your privacy and believe developer tools should operate with complete transparency.

---

### 1. 100% Offline & Local Operation
VibeAutoEnter runs entirely on your local machine:
- **No Telemetry**: We do not collect analytics, crash logs, or diagnostic reports.
- **No Cloud Communication**: The app never connects to external servers, APIs, or cloud infrastructure.
- **No Prompt Logging**: Any terminal output, code, or command inspected via macOS Accessibility APIs is processed strictly in-memory in real time to detect confirmation prompts. No keystrokes, terminal contents, or prompts are saved to disk or transmitted anywhere.

---

### 2. Permissions & How They Are Used
- **macOS Accessibility (`AXUIElement`)**:
  Used solely to detect confirmation prompts (such as `[y/N]`, `(y/n)`, `[1-3]`, and completion dialogs) and to automate pressing Enter or typing `"y"` into designated developer tools (Terminal, Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code).
- **AppleEvents Automation**:
  Used to bring the active terminal window to focus when auto-confirming interactive prompts.

---

### 3. Third-Party Data Sharing
Because VibeAutoEnter does not collect or transmit any data, we do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third parties.
